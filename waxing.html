Waxing

I offer both hard and soft waxing treatments. Soft wax is applied thinly and removed with a paper strip, ideal for larger areas such as the legs and arms. Hard wax is a peel-off wax applied thickly with a spatula and removed with fingers — used for sensitive areas such as eyebrows, upper lip, underarms, bikini line and Brazilian waxing. Hard wax is less painful and grabs shorter hair, eliminating the need to wax the same area twice.
