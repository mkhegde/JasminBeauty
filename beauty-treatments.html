Beauty Treatments

Facial treatment: cleanses, exfoliates and nourishes to promote clear, well-hydrated skin. A facial involves a deep cleanse, exfoliation, steam, blackhead removal, facial massage, a 10–15 minute mask specifically prepared for your skin type, and finally toning & moisturising. Luxury facial includes an extended face, neck, shoulder and arm massage.

Organic Body Polish/Facial: rejuvenates and helps tone, massaging on to smooth and refresh with exfoliating cranberry seed. Neal's Yard Remedies products are used.

Manicure/Pedicure: improves hand & foot appearance. There are two types — Standard and Luxury. Standard involves cuticle work, exfoliation and nail/toe paint. Luxury includes Standard plus a foot/hand mask, paraffin wax treatment with heated mitts/booties, and a hand/foot massage.
